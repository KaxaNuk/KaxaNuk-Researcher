# Portfolio rules

> **Yours, in your own words** — the rules you hold your portfolio by, one per line. `brief` reads
> this file and, under *Rules to look at*, quotes a rule the day's news bears on, word for word and
> with no verdict on it. It never says buy, sell, trim, add or hold, and never computes a weight, a
> P&L, a return or a risk figure: those come from the engines the project names. A heading that
> still holds its angle-bracketed prompt says nothing yet, and is skipped. This folder is
> gitignored, so it stays on this machine. Delete this note once you start.

## What this portfolio is for

<The goal, how long the money can stay invested, and what you compare it against — a savings rate,
an index, a mix.>

## What I hold, and what I never hold

<The kinds of asset you hold, and anything you exclude — a sector, a country, a kind of company.>

## How much in one place

<The most any one holding, sector or country may take of the whole, in your words.>

## When I buy or add

<What has to be true before money goes in — the evidence you need, the price, the moment.>

## When I sell or trim

<What makes you get out — a reason that no longer holds, a limit reached, a date, a loss you set in
advance.>

## When I look again

<How often you review the whole, and what makes you look sooner — a date, an earnings report, a
limit crossed.>

## What I never do

<The mistakes you never want to repeat, one per line.>
