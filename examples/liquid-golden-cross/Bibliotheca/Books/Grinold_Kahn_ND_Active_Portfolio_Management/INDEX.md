## What this book does not settle

For claim 4: it gives the band as a cost rule and the turnover rule of thumb, both from alphas fed
to an optimiser, and it prices neither for a screen that trades to a full target when it fires. Its
evidence stops in the late 1990s and reports no measured costs beyond a calibration of about 2
percent round trip. The half-life of this rule's signal, which is what its chapter 13 says a band
costs, is a number this repository has to measure itself.
