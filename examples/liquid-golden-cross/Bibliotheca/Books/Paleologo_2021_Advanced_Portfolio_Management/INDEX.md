## What this book does not settle

For claim 1: it separates momentum from trend following but tests no trend rule, so whether a
moving-average filter earns anything is not in it. For claim 2: it gives the sizing rules, the
mechanism by which the simplest one wins, and the machinery that would measure what a weighting
earns, but it prices no equal-weighted screen and no rule after costs; claim 2's number waits on a
weighting run beside this book. Its empirical work is the author's own simulation, and nothing in
it replicates a published factor.
