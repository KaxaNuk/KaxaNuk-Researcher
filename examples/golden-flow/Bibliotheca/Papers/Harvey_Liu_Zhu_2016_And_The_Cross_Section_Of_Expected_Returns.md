# Harvey, Liu & Zhu (2016) — *… and the Cross-Section of Expected Returns*

## What it says

Catalogues hundreds of published factors and argues that, after adjusting for the multiple testing
implied by the size of the search, a t-statistic near 2.0 is far too weak a bar. A newly proposed
factor needs something closer to 3.0 to be credible.
