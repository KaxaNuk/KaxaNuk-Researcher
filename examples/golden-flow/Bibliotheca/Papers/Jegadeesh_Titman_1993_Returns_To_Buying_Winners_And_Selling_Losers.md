# Jegadeesh & Titman (1993) — *Returns to Buying Winners and Selling Losers*

## What it says

The paper that made momentum mainstream. Buying three-to-twelve-month winners and selling losers
earned significant abnormal returns over three-to-twelve-month holding periods, and the effect was
not explained by systematic risk or by delayed reaction to common factors.
