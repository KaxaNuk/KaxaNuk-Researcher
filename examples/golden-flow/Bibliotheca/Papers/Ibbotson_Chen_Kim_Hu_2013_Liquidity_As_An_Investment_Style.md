# Ibbotson, Chen, Kim & Hu (2013) — *Liquidity as an Investment Style*

## What it says

Argues liquidity deserves standing alongside size, value and momentum as an investment style, with
less-liquid equities outperforming more-liquid ones consistently across size and style groupings.
