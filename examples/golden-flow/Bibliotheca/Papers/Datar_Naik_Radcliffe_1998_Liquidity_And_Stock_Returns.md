# Datar, Naik & Radcliffe (1998) — *Liquidity and Stock Returns: An Alternative Test*

## What it says

Uses turnover as the liquidity proxy and finds returns decrease with turnover — the same direction
as Amihud, through a measure much closer to the dollar-volume rank Golden Flow actually uses.

## What it implies for Golden Flow

> Rewritten on 2026-10-07 for this copy, after FINDINGS_1.md reported.
>
> **Claim 2, the sizing: the closest analogue, pointing the other way.** Turnover is the published
> measure nearest to Golden Flow's traded value, and the paper finds it predicts lower returns.
>
> Among the fifty most traded members, the analyzer found the opposite sign in the window. The
> traded-value rank's information coefficient over a year is +0.0977, positive on 73.6% of dates,
> against −0.0494 before 2015 ([`RESULTS.md`](../../RESULTS.md), *Before any experiment*, row 8).
> The book agrees with the window: 20.33% a year weighted by traded value, against 14.53% equally
> weighted. A sign that flips at the window's edge is not settled by one window, so the paper keeps
> its place against claim 2.
